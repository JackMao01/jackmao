01 · THE COASTAL LINE

Follow the city from salt air to evening light.

07:20 · Harbour

Jagalchi자갈치

Begin while the market is still unmistakably a workplace. Crates move, counters fill, and breakfast arrives without theatre. Look first, ask before photographing, and let the people working set the pace.

10:50 · Hillside

Huinnyeoul흰여울

The city lifts above the sea in lanes, steps, and walls that catch the coastal light. Walk slowly enough to remember that this is also a neighbourhood, not only a viewpoint.

14:10 · Crossing

Yeongdo영도

Harbour infrastructure and new creative spaces sit close together here. The pleasure is in the contrast: working edges, compact cafes, steep roads, and water appearing between buildings.

18:30 · Shore

Gwangalli광안리

Finish where the city turns toward the bridge and the beach becomes an evening room. Stay through the shift from daylight to electric light; Busan changes register without losing the sound of the water.

02 · AT THE COUNTER

Let lunch be the route.

A market is not a checklist of dishes. Ask what is good today, order one thing at a time, and leave space for the recommendation you did not plan.

Field notes.

Read the incline

Busan’s map looks flatter than the city feels. Leave extra time where the route rises away from the water.

Group the coast

Neighbourhoods spread along the shoreline. Choose one side of the city for a day instead of racing between every beach.

Keep the evening open

The best final stop may be the patch of water nearest you when the light begins to change.
