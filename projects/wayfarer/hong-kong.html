01 · THE ROUTE

A day measured in crossings.

08:10 · Morning

Sheung Wan上環

Begin with dried-goods shops, old stair streets, and a breakfast counter before the office towers fully wake. The city introduces itself in close quarters and quick, practical exchanges.

11:45 · Late morning

Wan Chai灣仔

Follow the tram past wet markets, narrow restaurants, and glass offices. Old and new do not sit in separate districts here; they share the same block and often the same doorway.

15:25 · Afternoon

Sham Shui Po深水埗

Cross the harbour for fabric stores, electronics stalls, and streets that still work at neighbourhood scale. Move slowly enough to notice how much life is held inside one dense grid.

18:45 · Dusk

Tsim Sha Tsui尖沙咀

Finish at the harbour when ferries, skyline lights, and evening crowds share the water's edge. The crossing is the point: Hong Kong keeps changing depending on which shore is looking back.
