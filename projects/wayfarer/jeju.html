01 · THE ISLAND ROAD

Let the weather edit the itinerary.

07:40 · East

Seongsan성산

Arrive early enough to watch the light find the volcanic rim. If clouds erase the summit, the coast below still gives the morning a shape. The island rarely offers only one reason to stop.

11:30 · Inland

An Oreum오름

Choose one of Jeju’s small volcanic cones and walk it without treating the top as a deadline. Grass, stone, and wind change the view long before the path reaches its highest point.

15:00 · South

Seogwipo서귀포

The south gathers waterfalls, markets, harbour edges, and slower streets. Pick a compact radius, then let lunch and weather decide which direction remains possible.

18:20 · West

Aewol애월

Meet the last light on the western coast, but step away from the busiest viewpoint. A stone wall, a quiet lane, and a smaller patch of water can hold the evening just as well.

02 · THE WORKING FORECAST

Carry options, not certainty.

Wind and rain can redraw an island day quickly. Keep one indoor stop, one short walk, and one meal nearby. Changing the plan is part of reading Jeju well.

Field notes.

Distances are real

The island is larger than a map thumbnail suggests. Build each day around one coast or region.

Respect the stone

Walls, fields, and trails are part of lived and working landscapes. Stay on marked paths and leave gates as you find them.

Receive the table

When someone recommends what is fresh or local, let that generosity shape the meal.
