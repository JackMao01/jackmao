01 · THE ROUTE

A day measured in arrivals.

08:10 · Morning

Euljiro을지로

Begin where print shops, tool stores, and narrow coffee counters share the same blocks. The city is already working. Let breakfast happen at street level, before the polished version of Seoul wakes up.

11:40 · Late morning

Seochon서촌

Walk west of Gyeongbokgung into a neighbourhood that still feels residential. Low roofs interrupt the skyline; small galleries and lunch rooms reward the side street rather than the itinerary.

15:20 · Afternoon

Hannam한남

Cross into a denser mix of design, fashion, steep lanes, and quiet homes. Choose one exhibition, not five. Seoul becomes clearer when there is enough room between stops to notice the change in pace.

18:35 · Dusk

Hangang한강

Finish beside the river as bicycles, convenience-store dinners, and evening conversations settle into the same open space. This is not a finale. It is where the city exhales before the night begins.

Keep in your pocket

Field notes.

Move lightly

Transit makes distance feel smaller than it is. Group nearby stops and leave one part of the day deliberately open.

Look up and down

Seoul is stacked. A doorway, basement sign, or second-floor window may hold the place you were trying to find.

Leave the main road

The character of a neighbourhood often appears one turn away from its busiest street.

다음 역

The next station is never the whole story.
